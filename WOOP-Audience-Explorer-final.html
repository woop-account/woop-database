About these numbers

On file today is the count of consented moms in WOOP's database right now. WOOP is a B2B company: we recruit moms against client briefs, not across every age group all the time, so what is on file reflects the briefs we have already run. A mom with children in different age groups is counted in each group, and a mom can sit in more than one macro audience.

What WOOP can recruit is our capability for the micro audience you chose, if a matching brief came in. It is not a forecast of what will be acquired. Recruitment is estimated at the micro-audience level; nano audiences narrow today's file, not what we add.

Several nano audiences at once show a range: moms can sit in more than one, so the combined count falls between the largest single audience and the sum of all of them. WOOP can supply the exact de-duplicated number.
